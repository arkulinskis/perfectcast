Questions

Is it free?

Yes. PerfectCast is free, with no ads.

Which books are on PerfectCast?

New and upcoming romantasy releases. Members can suggest books and characters; an admin checks them before they go live.

Which photos can I use?

Photos of public figures that you're allowed to share. No private people, nothing explicit. Members can report a collage and admins remove what breaks the rules.

Why are photos so small?

To keep things fast and light, every photo is cropped on your device and saved at 600×750 pixels (a book cover at 600×900). Each member can keep up to 60 photos.

Who can see my collages?

Everyone: collages, captions and comments are public, with your display name. Your email is never shown. The Privacy & Terms page has the details.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in any modern browser, and you can add it to your home screen.
